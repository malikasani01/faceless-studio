## The channel

One sentence on the niche. One on the viewer. One on the promise.

## The rule

No new production while finished, unpublished videos exist. State the number: production pauses when the backlog reaches [n].

## The week

| Day | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Brief: what shipped, what it did, what is proposed, what needs a decision | Operator (scheduled) |
| Tuesday | Approved items move into production | Producer |
| [day] | Publish slot | Publisher |
| [day] | Publish slot | Publisher |
| Sunday | Numbers recorded, next week drafted | Analyst |

Keep it to two or three publishing slots. Predictable beats frequent.

## The roles

Not separate products. Scheduled tasks or on-demand sessions that share the `studio/` documents as memory.

- **Operator** — reads the state, decides the week, collects approvals. Never publishes.
- **Research** — keeps the title list alive from what people actually search for and what is working on the platform.
- **Writer** — turns an approved title into a script and a beat map.
- **Producer** — generates and assembles. Guarded by the backlog rule and the credit reserve.
- **Publisher** — schedules approved items, nothing else.
- **Analyst** — records what happened and feeds it back to the Operator.

## Approvals

One message a week. Numbered items. The user replies with the numbers and a plain verdict. Anything not mentioned is not approved.

## The autonomy ladder

| Level | What it covers | Earned when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Drafts, research, internal docs, private uploads | now |
| 1 | Captions and metadata, reposting an approved video elsewhere | 4 clean weeks |
| 2 | Publishing short videos on schedule | 8 clean weeks |
| 3 | The user's own voice or face, factual claims, spending | never |

A clean week: nothing published that the user would have changed. Not clean, counter resets.
