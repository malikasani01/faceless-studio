# Faceless Studio — how to install

A Claude skill that builds you a faceless video studio: your brand documents,
your first three videos, and the weekly rhythm that keeps it running.

## Before you start

You need a **Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan.** Custom skills cannot be
uploaded on the free plan.

For the video generation steps you also need **Higgsfield** and **Blotato**
connected. Without them the skill still runs: it writes every script, prompt and
schedule as files for you to paste in by hand, and tells you which step is manual.

## Install, in four clicks

1. Download `faceless-studio.zip`. **Do not unzip it.**
2. In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities**.
3. Find the **Skills** section and click **Upload skill**.
4. Choose the zip. It appears in your Skills list, switched on.

That's it.

## Use it

Start a new chat and say:

> Set up my faceless video studio

It takes over from there.

## What happens next

It asks you questions in three short rounds — your channel, your look, and what
you can realistically sustain. Answer honestly rather than ambitiously; the last
round in particular.

Then it writes four documents into a `studio/` folder. These are the real asset:
your brand and voice, your content pillars with twenty title ideas, your
repeatable formats, and your operating system. A fresh Claude chat can read those
four files and make a video that sounds exactly like you.

Then it produces your first three videos end to end, and sets up your weekly
rhythm.

Allow about ten minutes for the interview.

## What it will not do

It will not put you on autopilot on day one, and it will tell you why. Publishing
permission is earned over weeks.

It will also refuse to make new videos while you have finished ones you have not
published. That is the mistake that kills most of these channels, and the refusal
is deliberate.

## Cost

Generating images, video and voice costs real money through Higgsfield. The skill
estimates a batch before running it and stops if your balance is low.
